Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

Taj Mahal is in Agra. It is one of the seven wonders of the world.

2. Jaipur Palace

Jaipur is known as Pink City. It has beautiful palaces.

3. Goa Beach

Goa is famous for its beaches and nightlife.

4. Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is famous for backwaters and houseboats.

5. Manali

Manali is a hill station famous for snow and mountains.
