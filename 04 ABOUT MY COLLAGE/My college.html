About College

Chitkara University was established in 2010 under the Chitkara University Act by the Punjab Government.
It is located on the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway in Rajpura, Punjab.
The university was founded by Dr. Ashok Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara.
It offers courses in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, and Health Sciences.
The campus has modern infrastructure, labs, hostels, and a central library.
It is known for its excellent placements, industry tie-ups, and international exchange programs.

Facilities

Library
The library is a very important place for students. It is a quiet place where we can read books and study peacefully. There are many types of books like story books, educational books and newspapers in the library. It helps us to increase our knowledge and improve our reading habit. Every school and university should have a good library for students.


Laboratory
The laboratories at Chitkara University are very modern and well-equipped. There are separate labs for all departments like Computer Science, Mechanical, Pharmacy and Nursing. All labs have the latest machines, computers and safety equipment for practical learning. Students get to perform practicals under the guidance of experienced lab assistants and faculty. These labs help students to convert their knowledge into practical skills.


Cafeteria
The cafeteria of Chitkara University is a very popular place for all students. It is very clean, spacious and has a nice seating arrangement. It serves a variety of tasty and hygienic food like sandwiches, thalis, juices and snacks at affordable prices. Students love to spend their free time and lunch breaks in the cafeteria with friends. It is not just a food place but also a place to relax and chit-chat.
