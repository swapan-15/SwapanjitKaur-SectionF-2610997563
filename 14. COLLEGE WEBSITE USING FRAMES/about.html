About Us

1. Chitkara University was established in 2010 under the Chitkara University Act by the Punjab Government.
2. It is located on the Chandigarh-Patiala National Highway in Rajpura, Punjab.
3. The university was founded by Dr. Ashok Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara.
4. It offers courses in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, and Health Sciences.
5. The campus has modern infrastructure, labs, hostels, and a central library.
6. It is known for its excellent placements, industry tie-ups, and international exchange programs.
