CHITKARA UNIVERSITY

A Premier Institution for Higher Education and Research

1. Chitkara University is a private university located in Rajpura, Punjab, near Chandigarh.
2. It was established in the year 2010 by the Chitkara Educational Trust.
3. The university is known for its courses in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, and Design.
4. It has two campuses - one in Punjab and another in Himachal Pradesh.
5. Chitkara University has good industry connections and provides strong placement opportunities to its students.
